My Projects

Enuhri Hub

An upgraded take on this very portfolio, reimagined as an interactive retro CRT computer. Inside, you can open different apps: StudyQuest, UDM-RADAR, CyberDefender, this classic portfolio, and the ENUHRI Art Gallery, a walkable museum you explore by dragging, filled with artworks submitted by its own visitors.

CyberDefender

An 8-bit retro mobile cybersecurity simulation game built in Godot 4. Players navigate physical PCB hardware layers and hazard zones to neutralize malware archetypes like Trojans, Spyware, and Ransomware through turn-based incident response combat and hardcore system overload challenges.

UDM-RADAR

A role-based academic decision-support platform built for Universidad de Manila's College of Computing Studies. It uses a Decision Tree Regressor to project GWA and flag academic risk, then routes findings through a human-in-the-loop workflow across Student, Faculty, and Admin portals for review, notices, and acknowledgment.

StudyQuest

An AI-assisted learning platform that turns uploaded syllabi, modules, and reviewers into structured courses with chapter reviews, mixed assessments, and gamified progression. Built with Django and Gemini 3.6 Flash, with Pydantic-validated generation, server-graded quizzes, and a fully mobile-responsive study flow.

Enuhri Art Gallery

An interactive virtual museum where visitors walk through classical halls and view artwork submitted by users. Explore neoclassical rooms by dragging or swiping, inspect framed artworks with plaques, or enter the Artist Studio to paint on canvas and submit creations directly to the gallery.
